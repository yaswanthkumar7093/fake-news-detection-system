How TruthLens Works

Three independent AI layers analyze every piece of content

🤖

BERT AI Model

A BERT transformer model fine-tuned on thousands of real and fake news articles classifies content with high precision using deep semantic understanding.

Primary Layer — 55% weight
🔤

Linguistic Analysis

Scans for sensational language, clickbait patterns, excessive caps, emotional triggers, and lack of journalistic hedging words used in credible reporting.

Secondary Layer — 30% weight
🌐

Source Credibility

Cross-references the source domain against a curated database of trusted outlets (Reuters, BBC, AP) and known misinformation websites.

Tertiary Layer — 15% weight
